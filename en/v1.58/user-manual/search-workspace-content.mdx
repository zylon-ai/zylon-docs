---
title: Search workspace content
description: Find projects, chats, AI Documents, and files you have access to using Search.
icon: magnifying-glass
---

Use **Search** to find content you can access in your workspace:

- **Projects** by name.
- **Chats**, including personal and collaborative conversations.
- **AI Documents**, such as summaries. You can also search inside text documents created by the assistant.
- **Files**, including uploads and connector imports.

Press **Cmd/Ctrl-K** to open Search, then enter your search terms. Use the **Projects**, **Documents**, or **Chats** filters to narrow the results. Content matches show an excerpt and the project they belong to.

Results respect permissions: you only see content from projects you can access.
