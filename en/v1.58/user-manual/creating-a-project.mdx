Projects are the workspaces where chats, AI Documents, and a project-specific knowledge base live. When you create a project, it is **created in your private space by default** (visible only to you).

## Steps
1. Go to the sidebar and select New project.
2. Enter a name.
3. Create. You will be redirected to the project and the project will appear in the project list.

## Rename a project you own

1. Open the project's **…** menu in the sidebar and select **Project settings**.
2. Change **Project name** and select **Save**.
3. Check the updated name in the sidebar. Reopen **Project settings** to confirm that the new name was saved.

> **Tip:** You can **pin** projects to keep personal shortcuts. Pinning is a per‑user convenience and does not affect other users.
