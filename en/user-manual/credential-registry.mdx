The **Credential Registry** gives workspace operators a central view of the connector credentials used by users across the workspace and its projects. Use it to review who created a connection, where it is used, and when it was created.

![Credential Registry](/images/user-manual/credential-registry.png)

## What you can manage

Credential Registry includes connections for:

- External integrations, such as Confluence, SharePoint, Claromentis, File System, and Google Drive.
- MCP servers.
- Database connectors.

The registry is managed at the platform level, so operators can find credentials used across all available organizations and projects—including private or unmanaged projects—from one place. The registry does not display secret values.

Connections are created inside projects, not from the Credential Registry. The registry is a read-only administrative inventory for reviewing and controlling existing connections.

## Find a credential

Open **Credential Registry** from the platform area. Use the available filters to narrow the list by:

- **Organization**.
- **User Status**, to find credentials created by active or inactive users.
- **Date**, to select a creation-date range.
- **Search**, to search by the credential or connector name.

The table shows the connector name, organization, type, project, creator, creator status, and creation date. Select the arrow at the end of a row to open the credential details.

## Review credential details

The details panel includes:

- The credential name and type.
- The project where the connector is used.
- The user who created the credential and their current status.
- The creation date.
- The current credential status.

## Revoke a connector

To stop a connector from being used:

1. Open the credential details.
2. Select **Revoke Credentials**.
3. Review the confirmation message and select **Yes, revoke**.

Revoking credentials disables the connection for the users and projects that use it. It does not remove files or data that were already added through the connector. Existing content remains available in the workspace, but the connector cannot be used to access or ingest new content until it is configured again.

> Revocation affects the selected connector credential across its workspace usage. Confirm the organization, project, and creator details before revoking access.

Revoking a credential is different from deleting a project or removing previously ingested content. The registry action disables the connector; it does not erase workspace files or data.

## Access requirements

Credential Registry is available to authorized platform operators. If the feature is disabled for your platform, contact your Zylon administrator.
