---
title: Installing an organization Skill
description: Reuse a Skill your admin already published, without building it yourself.
icon: download
---

If your organization admin has published Skills at the organization level, you can add any of them to your project in a couple of clicks — no need to recreate one from scratch.

## Steps

1. Open your project and go to the **Skills** tab in the sidebar.
2. Click **New Skill**, then choose **Install from organization**.
3. Browse the list of Skills published by your admin.
4. Click **Install** next to the one you want.

![Browsing and installing a Skill from the organization library](/images/user-manual/skills/install-from-org-dialog.png)

The Skill now shows up in your project's Skills list, right alongside any Skills created directly in the project, and is ready to use in chat.

<Info>
An installed organization Skill is shared, not copied — if your admin updates or removes it, that change applies everywhere it's installed.
</Info>
