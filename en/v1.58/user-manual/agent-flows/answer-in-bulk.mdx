Paste a list of **questions** and point the flow at relevant files/folders in the Knowledge Base. The output is a **table** of **Question → Answer** with **citations** to the exact source passages.

- Ideal for RFPs, due diligence, vendor questionnaires.
- Export options include document/table formats (e.g., CSV).
